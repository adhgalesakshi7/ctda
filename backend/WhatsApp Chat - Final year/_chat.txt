I. General Manuscript Structure (Single-Column)
Title: 24-point Times New Roman, centered.
Author Information: 11-point Times New Roman, centered below the title. Includes names, affiliations, and email addresses.
Abstract: One paragraph (150-250 words), beginning with the word "Abstract" in boldface (or italicized and bold) followed by an em dash.
Keywords: 3-5 keywords or phrases listed for indexing.
Main Body (Single Column):
I. Introduction
II. Method
III. Results and Discussion
IV. Conclusion
Acknowledgment (Unnumbered)
References (Unnumbered, in IEEE citation style)

Paper Size: US Letter (8.5" x 11") or A4.
